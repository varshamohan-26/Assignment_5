# Power BI Sales Analysis Dashboard

## Overview
This assignment uses Microsoft Power BI to analyze sales performance, profitability, sales targets, monthly trends, and order distribution across categories, sub-categories, and geographical locations.

## Tools Used
- Microsoft Power BI Desktop
- DAX (Data Analysis Expressions)
- Data Modeling and Visualization

## Visualizations Created

### 1. Sales Target Achievement by Category
- **Visual:** Clustered Column Chart
- Compared sales amounts with sales targets across categories.

### 2. Maximum Profit Margin by Sub-Category
- **Visual:** Donut Chart
- Analyzed maximum profit margins across product sub-categories.

### 3. Monthly Sales Trend
- **Visual:** Line Chart
- Visualized sales trends over months.

### 4. Comparison of Profit and Quantity by Sub-Category
- **Visual:** Scatter Chart
- Compared profit and quantity across product sub-categories.

### 5. Comparison of Total Sales Amount and Target
- **Visuals:** Card and Multi-row Card
- Displayed total revenue, total sales target, and minimum target by category.

### 6. Sales Performance Matrix
- **Visual:** Matrix
- Compared revenue and sales targets across categories and months.

### 7. Geographic Sales Analysis
- **Visual:** Map
- Visualized sales distribution by city.

### 8. Sales Distribution by Sub-Category
- **Visual:** Treemap
- Represented total revenue across product sub-categories.

### 9. Order Count Analysis by State
- **Visual:** Funnel Chart
- Displayed order counts across states.

## DAX Measures and Calculated Columns

- **Total Revenue:** Calculates total revenue using revenue per order.
- **Order Count:** Counts distinct orders.
- **YTD Revenue:** Calculates year-to-date revenue.
- **Average Profit in Delhi:** Calculates average profit for Delhi.
- **Minimum and Maximum Order Dates:** Identifies the earliest and latest order dates.
- **Revenue per Order:** Calculates revenue by multiplying amount by quantity.
- **Profit Margin:** Calculates profit as a percentage of amount.
- **Category Type:** Combines category and sub-category.
- **Sales Category:** Classifies sales as above or below average.

## Data Modeling
- Created a Calendar table with year, month, quarter, and day attributes.
- Established relationships between the Calendar, Orders, Order Details, and Sales Target tables.
- Used DAX measures and calculated columns to support sales analysis.

## Outcome
Created a Power BI report to explore sales performance, profitability, target achievement, monthly trends, and geographical order distribution.
